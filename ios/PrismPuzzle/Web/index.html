How to play & privacy

Cube: match all nine stickers on each face. Drag the background to orbit; swipe a face horizontally for a quarter-turn. Use labeled controls for precise turns. A prime (′) means counterclockwise, viewed facing that side.

Lights out: switch off every light. A tap flips that light and its neighbors. Slide nine: arrange 1–8 in order, leaving the bottom-right square empty.

Hints apply one guaranteed step back along your play path. They are unlimited, local, and may not be the shortest solution. Progress saves only on this device. No accounts, ads, analytics, or data collection.
