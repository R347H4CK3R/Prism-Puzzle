How to play & privacy

Cube and pocket cube: match each face. Drag a face to turn its layer, or choose Look around to rotate the whole object. Labeled controls offer precise turns. A prime (′) is counterclockwise viewed facing that side.

Block assembly: lift, rotate and fit the original studded pieces into each tray without overlap. Select a piece then tap a board square as an alternative to dragging. Tower: move all four disks to the right post, one at a time, never placing a larger disk on a smaller one.

Sliding puzzles: drag or tap a tile beside the gap to arrange the numbers in order. Lights out: turn off every light; tapping flips it and its neighbors.

Unlimited hints work entirely offline. Cube and sliding hints retrace your play path; Lights Out reverses pending taps; tower hints find a shortest route; assembly hints find a fit and may lift a blocking piece. Hints are not always the shortest solution. Progress stays on this device. No accounts, ads, analytics or data collection.
