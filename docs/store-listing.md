# Proposed store listing

Name: Prism Puzzle
Subtitle: Twist. Think. Find your flow.
Short description: An offline cube and puzzle collection with unlimited free hints.

## Description
Make a little room for play.

Prism Puzzle brings three satisfying logic games into one calm, colorful space. Turn a classic 3×3 twist cube, chase the final glow in Lights Out, or slide eight tiles into perfect order.

• A cube you can hold: drag to look around, swipe a face to turn, or use precise labeled controls.
• A helping hand, every time: unlimited hints with no coins, timers, ads, or purchases.
• Your own pace: save your progress on your device and return when you like.
• Play anywhere: no account or connection needed in the installed app.

Hints retrace your puzzle's move path one step at a time. They always offer a route home, without promising the fewest moves.

Three puzzles. One small moment of focus.

## Monetization proposal, not configured
A one-time paid download can fund the app while keeping every hint free. No pricing or payment integration has been set. Owner chooses launch price and territories before submission.
